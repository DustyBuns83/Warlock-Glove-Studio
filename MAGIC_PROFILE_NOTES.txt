Magic / Rainbow standby — 4500K Prism Shimmer v1

Design target:
- 1.25 second full breathing cycle: 50% -> 90% -> 50%
- Approximately 80% of LEDs remain near a 4500 K white base
- Approximately 20% become smoothly changing rainbow shimmer pixels
- Shimmer pixels follow the same global brightness pulse; they do not flash independently
- Random targets crossfade instead of snapping to reduce digital-looking flicker
- Speed slider scales the pulse/shimmer timing; Brightness scales the entire effect

The existing Magic Cast routine is intentionally unchanged in this revision.
Correct_V4_MASTER.ino is preserved untouched as the firmware reference.
